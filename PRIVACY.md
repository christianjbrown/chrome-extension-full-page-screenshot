# Privacy

Christian's Full Page Screenshot doesn't collect, store or send any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no servers: everything it does happens in your own browser.

When you click the button, it scrolls the page in that tab and captures each screenful, then joins them into one PNG on your computer. The PNG goes straight to your downloads folder.

Nothing it sees leaves your computer, and none of it is shared with anyone.

Questions? Open an issue at https://github.com/christianjbrown/chrome-extension-full-page-screenshot/issues.
